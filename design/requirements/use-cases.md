# Use Cases

## UC-001 — Explore a game situation

**Actor:** 10U player with a coach or parent  
**Goal:** See where all players should move when the puck changes location or ownership.

1. The actor chooses Blue, loose puck, or Orange.
2. The actor clicks or drags the puck to a location.
3. While dragging, the carrier or chaser follows the puck and teammates shift proportionally.
4. The app classifies the release point into a teaching area while preserving its exact coordinates.
5. Prior positions and dashed paths compare the completed move with its starting formation.

Moving within the same teaching area creates a new comparison because the exact player placements also change.

## UC-002 — Study one role

The actor selects a circle or responsibility row. The matching player is highlighted, and the table explains that role for the current scenario.

## UC-003 — Operate without a pointer

The actor tabs to possession/team controls and the rink. Arrow keys select neighboring area centers. A live region announces possession and the selected team's relative zone and lane.

## UC-004 — Draw a coaching note

The coach enables markup mode and draws one or more freehand marks on the rink. The app renders a smoothed translucent stroke beneath the players. Clear markup removes all strokes; disabling markup mode restores puck and player interaction.
