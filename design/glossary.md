# Glossary

| Term           | Meaning                                                                                |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Teaching area  | One of nine stable combinations of longitudinal rink zone and upper/middle/lower lane. |
| Team phase     | In possession, out of possession, or loose puck from one team's perspective.           |
| Relative zone  | Defensive, neutral, or offensive zone from one team's attacking direction.             |
| Formation      | The six displayed placements and responsibilities for one team in a scenario.          |
| Ghost          | A low-opacity marker at a player's immediately previous placement.                     |
| Strong side    | The side of the ice containing the puck.                                               |
| Above the puck | Positioned closer to the team's defensive goal than the puck.                          |
| Micro-movement | A bounded placement adjustment driven by the puck's exact in-area position.            |
| Markup stroke  | An ephemeral smoothed marker path drawn by a coach on the rink.                        |
